## Archived consequence evidence

The supplied `Pacific_Signal_Room_enhanced.zip` contains an earlier climate-justice implementation and its supporting validation note. This expansion will therefore show reported consequence coverage as a bounded, inspectable layer—not a Pacific-wide impact ranking.
